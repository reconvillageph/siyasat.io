What is Siyasat Linux?

Siyasat Linux is a specialized distribution designed for Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) investigations, catering to the needs of threat intelligence analysts and other intelligence professionals. Built on the foundations of Debian, it provides a comprehensive suite of tools and a customized environment to streamline investigation workflows.
